# Expense Tracker

Expenses have two scopes:
- Home
- Personal

Frequencies:
- One-off
- Monthly
- Yearly

Monthly cost:
- Monthly expenses contribute their full amount.
- Yearly expenses contribute amount / 12.
- One-off expenses dated in the next calendar month are included in the next-month projection.

The dashboard shows:
- Home monthly cost
- Personal monthly cost
- Combined monthly cost

Every expense can be edited or deleted.
